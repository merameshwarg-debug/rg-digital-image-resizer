About RG Digital Image Resizer

Welcome to RG Digital Image Resizer, a free online image utility website designed to make common image editing tasks simple, fast and convenient.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to provide easy-to-use browser-based tools that help users prepare images for websites, online forms, documents, social media and everyday digital use.

Our Tools

  • Resize images by width and height
  • Compress images to reduce file size
  • Resize images approximately to a target KB size
  • Convert between JPG, PNG and WebP formats
  • Adjust image DPI settings
  • HD 2× and 4× image upscaling
  • Background removal and background replacement
  • Hair and edge refinement tools

Easy to Use

Our tools are designed with a simple interface so that users can work with images directly from a mobile phone, tablet or computer without needing advanced image-editing software.

Privacy Focus

Where image processing is performed in your browser, the selected image is processed locally on your device. Please read our Privacy Policy for more information about website privacy and third-party services.
