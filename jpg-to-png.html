JPG and PNG are two popular image formats used for digital images. PNG can be useful when you need an image format that supports transparency or lossless image storage.

How to Convert JPG to PNG

  1. Open the RG Digital Image Tool.

  3. Choose PNG as the output format if the option is available.

  5. Download your converted PNG image.

Why Convert JPG to PNG?

  • PNG supports transparent backgrounds.
  • PNG uses lossless compression.
  • Useful for graphics, logos and images requiring transparency.
  • Easy to use for websites and digital projects.
  • Conversion can be done from a mobile phone or computer.
Note: Converting a JPG to PNG does not restore image quality that was already lost during JPG compression. The resulting PNG may also have a larger file size.
