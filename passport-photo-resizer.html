Resize Passport Photo Online

Passport and application forms may require a photograph with specific dimensions, aspect ratio, DPI or file-size limits. Before uploading a photo, always check the exact requirements provided by the relevant website or organization.

How to Resize a Passport Photo

  2. Open the RG Digital Image Resizer.

Common Passport Photo Requirements

Requirement What to Check
Photo Size Follow the exact dimensions specified by the application.
Aspect Ratio Use the width-to-height ratio required by the form.
File Format Usually JPG/JPEG or another format specifically requested.
File Size Check the maximum or minimum KB/MB requirement.
Background Follow the background requirement of the application.
DPI Use the specified DPI when the application provides a DPI requirement.
Important: There is no single passport-photo size that applies to every website or application. Requirements can differ between passports, visas, exams, government forms and other applications. Always follow the official instructions for your specific application.

Tips for a Good Passport Photo

  • Use a clear and sharp photograph.
  • Keep the face clearly visible.
  • Follow the required background instructions.
  • Do not crop the photograph incorrectly.
  • Check the required dimensions before uploading.
  • Check the final file format and file size.
