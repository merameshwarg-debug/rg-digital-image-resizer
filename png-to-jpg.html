PNG and JPG are widely used image formats. JPG is often useful when you need a smaller image file for websites, online forms, documents or sharing.

Why Convert PNG to JPG?

  • JPG files are often smaller than PNG files.
  • Useful for websites and online forms.
  • Easy to share and store.
  • Can help reduce image file size.
  • Convenient for mobile and desktop users.
Important: JPG does not support transparent backgrounds. If your PNG contains transparency, the transparent area may appear with a solid background after conversion.
