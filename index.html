Frequently Asked Questions

How can I reduce an image size?

You can reduce image size by changing its dimensions, adjusting compression quality, changing the image format, or using a combination of these methods.

Can I resize an image to 20KB?

Yes. RG Digital has a dedicated page for preparing images around 20KB. Always check the final file size and the exact requirements of your application.

Can I compress a JPG image?

Yes. JPG images can generally be compressed to reduce their file size. Excessive compression may reduce image quality.

Can I convert JPG to PNG?

Yes. Use the JPG to PNG converter page to prepare an image in PNG format.

What DPI should I use?

DPI requirements depend on the specific application or printing requirement. Always use the DPI value specified by the relevant organization.

Important Information

Image requirements are different for different websites, government services, examinations, forms and applications. RG Digital does not guarantee acceptance of an image by any particular website. Always follow the official instructions of the website or organization where you are submitting your image.
